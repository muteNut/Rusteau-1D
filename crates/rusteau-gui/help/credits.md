## Numerical methods

The numerical engine of Rusteau-1D is a port of the HYDRUS-1D code by J. Šimůnek, M. Šejna, H. Saito, M. Sakai and M. Th. van Genuchten [@simunek2009; @simunek2013]. The equations and methods come from the publications cited in this help ([References](references)).

## Pedotransfer functions

The Rosetta network weights come from rosetta-soil of the USDA-ARS U.S. Salinity Laboratory (CC0 1.0) [@rosettaSoil]; the models are those of Schaap et al. [@schaap2001; @schaap2004] and Zhang and Schaap [@zhangSchaap2017].

The interface is built with egui and eframe (Rust).
