Reference

References

The equations and methods of Rusteau-1D come from the HYDRUS-1D code (version 4.08) and its technical manual Šimůnek et al. (2009), which the numerical engine ports, and from the publications below. Each entry names what Rusteau-1D bases on it. Citations in the help topics link to this list; each topic also ends with the sources it cites.

Basis for: Random sequential adsorption (RSA) blocking function.

Akaike, H. (1974). A new look at the statistical model identification. IEEE Transactions on Automatic Control 19(6): 716–723. doi:10.1109/TAC.1974.1100705

Basis for: Penman–Monteith equation: radiation terms, aerodynamic and canopy resistance, vapor pressure, psychrometric constant; LAI from the crop height.

Ångström, A. (1924). Solar and terrestrial radiation. Quarterly Journal of the Royal Meteorological Society 50(210): 121–126. doi:10.1002/qj.49705021008

Basis for: Solar radiation from the relative sunshine duration, Rs = (as + bs n/N) Ra.
