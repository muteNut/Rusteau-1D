Reference

Credits and license

Numerical methods

The numerical engine of Rusteau-1D is a port of the HYDRUS-1D code by J. Šimůnek, M. Šejna, H. Saito, M. Sakai and M. Th. van Genuchten Šimůnek et al. (2009); Šimůnek et al. (2013). The equations and methods come from the publications cited in this help (References).

Pedotransfer functions

The Rosetta network weights come from rosetta-soil of the USDA-ARS U.S. Salinity Laboratory (CC0 1.0) rosetta-soil; the models are those of Schaap et al. (2001); Schaap et al. (2004) and Zhang and Schaap (2017).

Interface

The interface is built with egui and eframe (Rust). The figures are drawn with tiny-skia (BSD-3-Clause license); glyph outlines are read with ab_glyph and ttf-parser (Apache-2.0 / MIT licenses), PNG files are written with the png crate (MIT / Apache-2.0).
