Theory and equations

Hargreaves equation

When only air temperatures are available, the potential evapotranspiration can be estimated with the Hargreaves equation Hargreaves and Samani (1985); Hargreaves (1994):

ETp = 0.0023 Ra (Tm + 17.8) √(Tmax − Tmin)
SymbolMeaning
ETppotential evapotranspiration [mm/d], converted to the project's units
Raextraterrestrial radiation, expressed as equivalent evaporation (Ra [MJ/m²/d] × 0.408) [mm/d]
Tmmean daily air temperature, (Tmax + Tmin)/2 [°C]
Tmax, Tminmaximum and minimum daily air temperature [°C]

Ra follows from the latitude of the site and the day of the year Allen et al. (1998). The temperature range √(Tmax − Tmin) stands for the cloudiness: clear days have a large range and high radiation. ETp is partitioned into evaporation and transpiration with the soil cover fraction (partitioning).

The Hargreaves equation is recommended by FAO-56 when solar radiation, humidity and wind data are missing Allen et al. (1998); it tends to overestimate under humid and underestimate under windy, dry conditions.
