Results (post-processing)

Run-time information

The page shows how the calculation went, as Run Time Information in HYDRUS-1D (file Run_Inf.out).

The header gives the number of time levels, the duration of the calculation and the cumulative number of iterations, followed by the messages of the run (e.g. why it stopped). The plots show against time (Peclet and Courant numbers only with solute transport):

PlotMeaning
time step Δtthe adaptive time step (time step control)
iterations per stepiterations of the water flow; many steps at the maximum mean convergence problems
maximum Peclet numberPe = |q| Δz / (θ D) of the solute transport (discretization)
maximum Courant numberCr = |q| Δt / (θ R Δz)

Small time steps over long periods point to difficult conditions (infiltration into very dry soil, ponding, sharp layer contrasts); see questions and problems.
