Input (pre-processing)

Neural network prediction (Rosetta)

The dialog predicts the van Genuchten parameters θr, θs, α, n and Ks from soil data with the Rosetta pedotransfer functions Schaap et al. (2001); Zhang and Schaap (2017), as the Rosetta Lite dialog of HYDRUS-1D. It opens from Neural network prediction (Rosetta)… on the page Materials & parameters and from Rosetta… next to the soil catalog of the parameter estimation (initial estimates).

Input data

Rosetta is hierarchical: the more data, the better the prediction.

ModelInput
H1 Textural classthe USDA textural class; gives the average parameters of the class in the Rosetta database
H2 SSCsand, silt and clay [%] (sum 99–101 %)
H3 SSC + BDand the bulk density [g/cm³] (0.5–2)
H4 SSC + BD + θ33and the water content at 33 kPa (field capacity)
H5 SSC + BD + θ33 + θ1500and the water content at 1500 kPa (wilting point)

The USDA texture class of the entered fractions is shown below the inputs.

Models

VersionDescription
Rosetta 3 (default)One network for the retention parameters and Ks, recalibrated with weights from the uncertainty of the fitted retention curves and 1000 bootstrap members Zhang and Schaap (2017).
Rosetta 2Rosetta 1 with the retention parameters corrected after Schaap et al. (2004).
Rosetta 1The models of Rosetta Lite in HYDRUS-1D Schaap et al. (2001).

All are ensembles of neural networks trained on bootstrap samples; the prediction is the ensemble mean, shown with its standard deviation. Geometric means uses 10 to the mean of the logarithms of α, n and Ks instead of the arithmetic means. The network weights are those of the Python package rosetta-soil; the unit tests reproduce its predictions.

Result

The prediction is given in cm and days, with K0 and L of the conductivity function with a matching point Schaap and Leij (2000) for information. Accept writes θr, θs, α, n and Ks, converted to the project's units, into the material (or the initial estimates); l stays.

Pedotransfer functions give approximate parameters for a soil like the soils of their database (mostly temperate agricultural soils). Measured retention data or a parameter estimation are better when available.

See also: pedotransfer functions.
