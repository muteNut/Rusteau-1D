Solute transport: temperature and water-content dependence, colloids

The page appears when the temperature dependence, the water-content dependence of reactions or the colloid model is switched on (page Solute transport: general). It corresponds to the dialogs Temperature Dependence of Reaction Parameters, Water Content Dependence of Reaction Parameters and the attachment parameters of the Virus, Colloid, Bacteria Transport Model of HYDRUS-1D.

Activation energies

Per solute, the activation energy Ea [J/mol] of every transport and reaction parameter (Dw, Dg, Ks, η, β, Henry, μ, γ, μ0, ω). A parameter a at temperature T is a(T) = a(Tref) exp[Ea (T − Tref) / (R T Tref)] with the reference temperature 20 °C Stumm and Morgan (1981); 0 = no temperature dependence. Typical activation energies of degradation are 40–80 kJ/mol (theory).

Water-content dependence

Walker's formula Walker (1974): rate = rateref (θ/θref)B for θ < θref, with the exponent B (≈ 0.7) and the reference pressure head href whose water content is θref, for each of the nine rate coefficients.

Trapezoidal tables (MoistDep.in): per material, solute and reaction, the rate factor is ReacMin0 below θ0, rises linearly to 1 at θ1, stays 1 up to θ2 and falls to ReacMin1 at θ3 (θ2 = 0: no reduction). HYDRUS-1D writes but does not use MoistDep.in; Rusteau uses it (theory).
