Projects and files

Output files

File → Export results (CSV)… (and the command line program) write the results as comma-separated files with a header line. The files correspond to the output files of HYDRUS-1D Šimůnek et al. (2009). All values are in the project's units; signs follow HYDRUS (fluxes positive upward, i.e. negative values enter the soil at the surface and leave the profile at the bottom).

T_LEVEL.csv – time-level information

One line per time step (or every n-th step, Time & output).

ColumnMeaning
time, dt, tleveltime, time step, number of the time level
iter_w, it_cumiterations of the water flow in this step, cumulative iterations
kod_top, kod_botboundary condition code at the top and bottom in this step (positive: head, negative: flux)
rTop, rRootpotential surface flux and potential transpiration [L/T]
vTop, vRoot, vBotactual surface flux, actual transpiration, actual bottom flux [L/T]
sum_rTop … sum_vBotcumulative values of the fluxes [L]
hTop, hRoot, hBotpressure head at the surface, mean head of the root zone, head at the bottom [L]
runoff, sum_runoffsurface runoff [L/T] and its cumulative value [L]
volumewater volume in the profile per unit area [L]
sum_infil, sum_evapcumulative infiltration and evaporation [L]
precip, snow_layerprecipitation rate, thickness of the snow layer (water equivalent)
per solutecvTop, cvBot (solute fluxes), sum_cvTop, sum_cvBot, sum_cvCh0, sum_cvCh1 (cumulative zero- and first-order reactions), cTop, cRoot, cBot, cvRoot, sum_cvRoot, sum_cvNEql, cGWL, cRunOff, sum_cRunOff
temp_top, temp_bottemperatures at the top and the bottom [°C]
peclet, courantmaximum Peclet and Courant numbers of the solute transport
cum_w_transfcumulative water transfer between the domains (dual porosity / permeability)

NOD_INF.csv – profiles at the print times

ColumnMeaning
time, node, depthprint time, node number (from the top), depth [L]
head, thetapressure head [L], water content [–]
K, Chydraulic conductivity [L/T], water capacity [1/L]
flux, sinkwater flux [L/T], root water uptake [1/T]
kappabranch of the hysteresis model (−1 drying, +1 wetting)
v_over_Ksflux relative to Ks of the top node
temptemperature [°C]
h_matrix, th_matrixhead and water content of the matrix or the immobile region (dual porosity / permeability)
per soluteconc (liquid), sorb (sorbed or immobile), conc_matrix, flux_conc

OBS_NODE.csv – observation nodes

time, node, head, theta, temp, flux, h_matrix, th_matrix and per solute conc, conc_matrix, flux_conc, one line per time step and observation node.

BALANCE.csv – mass balance

time, area (length of the region), volume, inflow, h_mean, top_flux, bot_flux, WatBalT (absolute error [L]) and WatBalR_percent (relative error [%]) at the print times (mass balance).

A_LEVEL.csv – atmospheric records

Cumulative fluxes and boundary heads at the end of every atmospheric record: time, sum_rTop, sum_rRoot, sum_vTop, sum_vRoot, sum_vBot, hTop, hRoot, hBot.

T_LEVEL1.csv – extended fluxes

Written with the option Write extended flux table T_Level1: potential and actual precipitation, evaporation, surface flux, infiltration, transpiration, bottom flux, runoff, their cumulative values, the storage, the snow layer and the liquid and vapor parts of the surface flux.

METEO.csv and Meteo.out

With meteorological data: the potential evapotranspiration and its components, the radiation terms and, with the energy balance, the surface energy fluxes. Meteo.out has the fixed-width layout of HYDRUS-1D.

IRRIG.csv

With triggered irrigation: one line per irrigation event (event, t_trigger, t_start, t_end, amount).

Fit.out

The results of a parameter estimation are written with Save Fit.out… on the page Parameter estimation results, in the layout of HYDRUS-1D 4.17.
